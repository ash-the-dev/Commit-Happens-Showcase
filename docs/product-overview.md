# Product Overview

## Positioning

Commit Happens is website-health software for operators who do not want a pile of disconnected SEO, analytics, and uptime tools.

Tagline:

> Your Website Has Warning Lights. We Translate Them.

Brand pink: `#f13a80`

Technical findings are part of the product. They are not the whole product. The job is to **understand and improve website health** — traffic, technical signals, search visibility, uptime, reputation, and guided action.

## Who it is for

- Small-business owners who need clarity, not jargon
- Creators and marketers watching properties they ship
- Agencies running multiple client sites from one account

Multi-site means one owner with many sites. This showcase does not claim a full team-RBAC / share-role model.

## Product capabilities

### Mission Control

Authenticated multi-site workspace for understanding website health.

### Website health checks

Public and authenticated flows that analyze a site and return plain-English results. Anonymous entry points support acquisition; connected sites deepen monitoring over time.

### Analytics

- First-party analytics owned by the product
- Optional **Google Analytics** connection for properties the customer already uses

### Search visibility

Optional **Google Search Console** connection so search signals sit next to technical findings.

### Monitoring

Uptime / availability and performance-oriented views so regressions are visible without constant manual checking.

### Guided Fix

Technical findings become understandable next actions. Workflow structure,
verification behavior, and issue-specific guidance remain private.

### AI assistance

Summaries and recommendations constrained by product data and workflows — not an unbounded chatbot.

### Reporting, trials, entitlements

Setup guidance and plan gates that move an account from signup → first site → first useful signal.

### Mobile

Native iOS and Android apps on the App Store and Google Play, sharing the web product surface.

## Product outcome

Customers connect a website, understand its health signals, and receive clear next
actions. Detailed onboarding, prioritization, scoring, and retention flows remain private.
