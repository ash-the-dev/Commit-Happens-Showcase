# Integrations

Product-level connections only. How they authenticate, store tokens, match
properties, or recover is not documented here.

## Google Analytics

Optional product feature. Customers can connect a property they already use so
those traffic signals sit next to first-party analytics in Mission Control.

## Google Search Console

Optional product feature. Customers can connect search visibility so those
signals sit next to technical findings.

## First-party analytics

The product can collect its own traffic and events, independent of third-party
tags. Collection and acceptance rules stay private.

## Technical analysis

Asynchronous website analysis produces site-scoped findings. Provider identity
and raw datasets remain private.

## AI

Used for summarization and guided assistance. Grounded in product-owned
context; prompts and raw outputs stay private.

## Commerce

Web and mobile purchases resolve to a unified access model. Reconciliation
and plan-gating logic remain private.

## Account messaging

Verification, billing, alerts, and lifecycle mail. Delivery configuration
stays private.
